# DWARVEN_ACTION_SQUAD -- IRON RECON OPS NETWORK

special ops dwarf squad trying to accomplish missions in enemy territory

### core resolution:

  - each dwarf has:
    - composure (CMP) stat and explosives (TNT) stat
    - 3 items with 2-3-4 uses
    - X stones
  
#### dice
d4-d6-d8 based on stat?

  roll 2 dice, assign one to WORK and one to BOOM. high work means clean operations, high boom means... very loud.
  loud actions raise the alarm clock, from 0 to 20.

health = stones

- explosives = roll a d6, that is the amount of d6 to roll for the explosive. all dwarves can add a d6 to help build the explosives
- the explosive dice do their thing but then go into the aftermath pool
- 6s explode

when players start a strike mission begin the alarm clock, everytime players do something roll the die and raise the alarm that much.. when players do something really loud etc step up the die. starts at d4. when hits 20 the alarm goes off.

### chargen

roll stats
roll looks
roll traits... inclusive!!
roll 3 skills, effectively your class
roll 3 items
roll 3 explosives
